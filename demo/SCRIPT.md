# OpenPatches — feature video narration

_Lengths measured from the espeak-ng scratch track. Regenerate with `node demo/make-voice.mjs && node demo/make-script.mjs`._

## The night sky — 9.4s

> OpenPatches turns the DX seven into a searchable library of sound. It runs entirely in your browser.

*On screen:* Empty night-sky canvas, then a bank of patches lands on it.

## Sound archeology — 11.9s

> Drop in a recording, and it works backwards from the audio to the patch that made it. Real DX seven parameters, out of a sound.

*On screen:* File, Import WAV. Progress modal, then a container of matched patches.

## Curation — 10.2s

> Collect what you find into cartridges of your own, and export them as sysex banks, ready for Dexed or for the hardware.

*On screen:* Drag a patch out of the cartridge onto the canvas, assign the cartridge to the voice slots, then the Export-as-SYX button.

## Morph and mutate — 11.7s

> Edit the operators by hand, morph between two patches to find what sits between them, or mutate one and keep its character.

*On screen:* Edit tab: Params, then Morph with the distance slider, then Random.

## The mixer — 5.3s

> Eight slots, eight voices, each one a patch you chose.

*On screen:* Mixer tab, patches assigned to slots, each with its colour.

## Patterns — 13.6s

> Write a pattern in the piano roll, or play it in from the keyboard. A pattern is just notes, so the same phrase can be a bass, or a bell.

*On screen:* Draw notes, Play, the loop running. Hold while it plays.

## Arranging — 10.7s

> Lay those patterns out as clips across the tracks of a section, and the whole thing plays together.

*On screen:* Section tab, clips on several tracks, section Play. Long hold: this is the music.

## Projects — 9.9s

> It is all one project, saved in your browser and never sent anywhere. Export it as a single file to pass it on.

*On screen:* File menu open, Export Project.

## Hardware — 11.8s

> And it talks to the real thing: send a bank to your synth over MIDI, and share a clock with it.

*On screen:* MIDI, Devices. Enable MIDI, the channel map, the clock switches.

*Insert:* Volca FM footage goes here, over the hold.

## Outro — 6.4s

> OpenPatches. Open the browser and start digging.

*On screen:* Back to the canvas, full of patches. Title card: openpatch dot e s.

# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

OpenPatches respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## 1. Data Collection
We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or IP addresses for tracking purposes.

## 2. Audio Processing
When you use the "Import .WAV" feature, your audio file is uploaded to our server for analysis. 
- Files are processed temporarily in memory or stored in a transient directory (`/exports`).
- We do not use your uploaded audio for training models or public distribution without your explicit consent.

## 3. Local Storage
OpenPatches stores your work (patches, canvas state, job history) locally in your browser using:
- **LocalStorage**: For patch metadata and UI state.
- **IndexedDB**: For temporary storage of uploaded audio binaries.

This data remains on your device and is not synchronized to our servers unless you explicitly export it.

## 4. Cookies
We do not use tracking cookies. We use standard browser storage for application functionality only.

## 5. Security
We use industry-standard security measures (HTTPS, security headers) to protect data in transit to our API.

## 6. Third-Party Links
The Service may contain links to GitHub or other external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

## 7. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via GitHub.
